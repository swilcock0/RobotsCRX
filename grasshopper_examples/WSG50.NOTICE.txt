WSG50.xml and WSG50.3dm define a Schunk WSG 50-110 gripper tool for the example
definition. To use it, copy both files to your Robots local library folder
(Documents\Robots by default).

The gripper body and jaw guide meshes come from the wsg_50_simulation package of
wsg50-ros-pkg by Nicolas Alt, Robotnik and Weiss Robotics
(https://github.com/nalt/wsg50-ros-pkg), declared BSD licensed, via the forks
https://github.com/inria-paris-robotics-lab/wsg50-ros-pkg and
https://github.com/swilcock0/wsg50-ros2-pkg. The adaptor plate on the body and
the BeamFinger fingers are by Sam Wilcock.

Changes: meshes placed in the Robots flange frame, converted to millimeters,
split into the body and two jaws, and colored.
