Changes made by tools/Robots.FanucCrx in the Robots repository: DH parameters,
joint ranges and joint speeds were derived from the URDF macros, and the COLLADA
visual and STL collision meshes were merged per link, placed at the zero pose
relative to the J2 height, and converted to millimeters. The COLLADA material
colors were stored as vertex colors.
